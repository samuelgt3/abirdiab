## D. Things checked and fine

- No page scrolls sideways at phone, tablet, laptop or big-monitor sizes, in English or Arabic.
- Every image has a description (alt text); every button and link has a name.
- Every page has exactly one main heading (except the invalid-gift page, #19); heading levels don't skip.
- Body text and grey small print have enough contrast on the cream background.
- Arabic pages mirror correctly; the booking window, prices ($ stays on the left of the number) and the account filters work in Arabic.
- Admin has search, filters, an empty state on every list, "are you sure?" before deleting content or subscribers, and a warning before leaving unsaved settings.
